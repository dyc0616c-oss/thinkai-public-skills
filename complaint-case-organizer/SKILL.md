# Complaint Case Organizer

1. Minimize personal data. Keep only fields needed to handle the complaint.
2. Separate the customer’s statement, verified facts, assumptions, and recommended actions.
3. Classify topic and urgency using user-provided rules; otherwise explain the default criteria.
4. Identify missing evidence, unclear requests, duplicate cases, deadlines, and escalation needs.
5. Draft a neutral response or next-step checklist when requested.
6. Do not decide guilt, automatically approve refunds, ban accounts, contact customers, or change external systems.
7. Mark high-impact actions as requiring authorized human review.
8. Use [references/case-template.md](references/case-template.md) for structured output.
