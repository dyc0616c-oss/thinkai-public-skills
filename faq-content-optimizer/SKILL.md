# FAQ Content Optimizer

1. Preserve the factual meaning and policy boundaries of the source answer.
2. Write one clear user question per entry.
3. Rewrite the answer in direct, polite, plain language.
4. Generate useful alternative questions and search phrases without adding new promises.
5. Identify duplicate or conflicting FAQ entries.
6. Flag claims, dates, fees, eligibility rules, or policy statements that require verification.
7. Do not connect to a customer-service backend, publish content, or send messages.
8. Require human review before finalized content is imported into a production knowledge base.

Use [references/faq-format.md](references/faq-format.md) for default output.
